Philips TVs

Remove the ads on a Philips TV

OwnYourTV switches off the ads and tracking your Philips TV came with, and gives it a calm home screen. From the remote, no computer. Nothing is deleted: one button puts everything back.

Downloader 8225348

What it switches off on Philips TVs

The app names Android shows, with what they do. OwnYourTV only ticks the ones checked on real TVs; you can untick any of them.

  • Net TV recommendationsorg.droidtv.nettvrecommender
  • Net TV app galleryorg.droidtv.nettv.market
  • Philips recommendationsorg.droidtv.recommendation
  • Store demoorg.droidtv.demome
  • Usage loggingorg.droidtv.usagelogger
  • Event loggingorg.droidtv.gel
  • Diagnostics sent to the makerorg.droidtv.candeebug
  • Google's sponsored rowscom.google.android.tvrecommendations

Before you start on a Philips

  • Philips Android TVs and Google TVs (made by TP Vision) add Net TV recommendations and their own logging on top of Google's.
  • On some models, Chromecast needs the setup wizard: OwnYourTV keeps what is needed so casting still works.
  • Ambilight, live TV and HDMI inputs are not touched.

Install it from your Philips TV

  1. Open the Downloader app (free, from your TV's Play Store).
  2. Type the code 8225348, install, open OwnYourTV.
  3. Follow the steps: about ten minutes, all with the remote.
