Sony TVs

Remove the ads on a Sony TV

OwnYourTV switches off the ads and tracking your Sony TV came with, and gives it a calm home screen. From the remote, no computer. Nothing is deleted: one button puts everything back.

Downloader 8225348

What it switches off on Sony TVs

The app names Android shows, with what they do. OwnYourTV only ticks the ones checked on real TVs; you can untick any of them.

  • Sony Select, promoted appscom.sony.dtv.sonyselect
  • Sony promotionscom.sony.dtv.promos
  • Store democom.sony.dtv.multiscreendemo
  • Store demo supportcom.sony.dtv.demosupport
  • Usage data sent to Sonycom.sony.dtv.da.service
  • Samba TV, viewing measurementtv.samba.ssm
  • Usage reportscom.sonyericsson.idd.agent
  • Google's sponsored rowscom.google.android.tvrecommendations

Before you start on a Sony

  • Sony Bravia TVs run Android TV or Google TV, often with Samba TV, which identifies what is on screen to measure viewing.
  • Store demo apps are still installed on many sets sold for home use: they are switched off too.
  • On a few Bravia models, the stock home has to stay: OwnYourTV then switches the ads off and leaves the home as it is.

Install it from your Sony TV

  1. Open the Downloader app (free, from your TV's Play Store).
  2. Type the code 8225348, install, open OwnYourTV.
  3. Follow the steps: about ten minutes, all with the remote.
