Hisense TVs

Remove the ads on a Hisense TV

OwnYourTV switches off the ads and tracking your Hisense TV came with, and gives it a calm home screen. From the remote, no computer. Nothing is deleted: one button puts everything back.

Downloader 8225348

What it switches off on Hisense TVs

The app names Android shows, with what they do. OwnYourTV only ticks the ones checked on real TVs; you can untick any of them.

  • Promotional messagescom.hisense.service.message
  • Hisense adscom.hisense.business.ad
  • Store modecom.hisense.storemode
  • Store democom.hisense.demonstrate
  • Logs sent to Hisensecom.hisense.logupload
  • Usage eventscom.hisense.evservice
  • Google's sponsored rowscom.google.android.tvrecommendations

Before you start on a Hisense

  • Many Hisense TVs run VIDAA, Hisense's own system, not Android: OwnYourTV cannot be installed on those.
  • On Hisense TVs with Android TV or Google TV, it switches off Hisense's messages, store demos and logging, and Google's sponsored rows.
  • To check: Settings › Device preferences › About. If you see Android TV OS or Google TV, it works.

Install it from your Hisense TV

  1. Open the Downloader app (free, from your TV's Play Store).
  2. Type the code 8225348, install, open OwnYourTV.
  3. Follow the steps: about ten minutes, all with the remote.
