Remove the ads on a TCL TV

OwnYourTV switches off the ads and tracking your TCL TV came with, and gives it a calm home screen. From the remote, no computer. Nothing is deleted: one button puts everything back.

Downloader 8225348

What it switches off on TCL TVs

The app names Android shows, with what they do. OwnYourTV only ticks the ones checked on real TVs; you can untick any of them.

  • On-screen stickers and bannerscom.tcl.esticker
  • Promotional messagescom.tcl.messagebox
  • TCL home recommendationscom.tcl.tv.tclhome_passive
  • TCL Channel, sponsored channelscom.tcl.channelplus
  • Promoted dashboardcom.tcl.dashboard
  • TCL account and usage trackingcom.tcl.usercenter
  • Usage statistics sent to TCLcom.tcl.bi
  • Logs sent to TCLcom.tcl.logkit
  • Google's sponsored rowscom.google.android.tvrecommendations

Before you start on a TCL

  • Most TCL TVs sold since 2021 run Google TV: OwnYourTV can switch the stock home off and put its own in its place.
  • On some Google TV models, the Wi-Fi network was saved by the setup wizard. The app then asks you once to forget it and join it again, so the TV keeps its internet.
  • Live TV, HDMI inputs and the TCL remote keep working: only ad and tracking apps are switched off.

Install it from your TCL TV

  1. Open the Downloader app (free, from your TV's Play Store).
  2. Type the code 8225348, install, open OwnYourTV.
  3. Follow the steps: about ten minutes, all with the remote.
