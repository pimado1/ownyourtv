Remove the ads on a Xiaomi TV

OwnYourTV switches off the ads and tracking your Xiaomi TV came with, and gives it a calm home screen. From the remote, no computer. Nothing is deleted: one button puts everything back.

Downloader 8225348

What it switches off on Xiaomi TVs

The app names Android shows, with what they do. OwnYourTV only ticks the ones checked on real TVs; you can untick any of them.

  • Web content and adscom.xm.webcontent
  • PatchWall recommendationscom.mitv.tvhome.atv
  • Mi Channel, sponsored channelscom.mitv.tvhome.michannel
  • Mi TV+ promoted contentcom.mitv.tvhome.mitvplus
  • Usage statistics sent to Xiaomicom.miui.tv.analytics
  • Xiaomi analyticscom.xiaomi.statistic
  • Floating promotional framecom.xiaomi.floatingframe
  • Google's sponsored rowscom.google.android.tvrecommendations

Before you start on a Xiaomi

  • Xiaomi TVs come with Google TV, or with PatchWall, Xiaomi's own home screen with promoted content.
  • Some Xiaomi models only accept their own home screen. On those, OwnYourTV says so, keeps the stock home and still switches the ads and tracking off.
  • Tested by an owner on a Xiaomi TV A Pro (MiTV-MZTU0) with Google TV 14: everything worked.

Install it from your Xiaomi TV

  1. Open the Downloader app (free, from your TV's Play Store).
  2. Type the code 8225348, install, open OwnYourTV.
  3. Follow the steps: about ten minutes, all with the remote.
