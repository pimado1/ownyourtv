Contact

One person makes OwnYourTV, in the open. Pick what fits: each way reaches me, and I answer.

Add my TV to the list The quickest way is the Share button at the end of the app. If your TV could not get that far, tell me its brand, its model and what worked here. Fill the form on GitHub → Report a problem A screen that looks off, a step that does not pass, an app that came back. A photo of the TV screen helps a lot. Report it on GitHub → Write to me A question, an idea, press or a partnership. French or English, both are fine. You can also reach me on X, @PimAoff. contact@ownyour.tv

Before writing

Most questions (Play Protect, the Wi-Fi step, updates, profiles and child mode) are answered in the questions on the home page. A GitHub account is free and takes a minute; it lets you follow the answer.
