Samsung, LG, Roku, Vizio: stop the tracking

OwnYourTV needs Android TV or Google TV. Your TV runs something else? It still watches what you watch: most smart TVs recognise what is on screen (ACR, automatic content recognition) and send it off for advertising. You can turn that off from the remote, in about five minutes.

Menu names change with the year and the country. If yours looks different, look for the words in bold.

Samsung

  1. Settings › All Settings › General & Privacy › Terms & Privacy (on 2018–2021 TVs: Settings › Support › Terms & Policy).
  2. Turn off Viewing Information Services and Interest-Based Advertisement.
  3. You don't talk to the TV? Turn off Voice Recognition Services too.

LG

  1. Settings › All Settings › General › System › Additional Settings: turn off Live Plus and Home Promotion.
  2. Settings › All Settings › Support (or General › System) › Privacy & Terms › User Agreements: untick Viewing Information and Interest-Based Recommendation.

Roku TV (TCL, Hisense, Sharp…)

  1. Settings › Privacy › Smart TV Experience: untick Use info from TV inputs.
  2. Settings › Privacy › Advertising: tick Limit ad tracking (or turn off Personalize ads).

Vizio

  1. Menu › Admin & Privacy: set Viewing Data to Off.
  2. In the same menu, turn off Advertising personalisation if it is offered.

Hisense, Toshiba (VIDAA)

  1. Settings › Support (or System) › Disclaimer & Privacy.
  2. Turn off Viewing Information Services and anything about personalised ads.

What this does, and does not do

It stops the TV reporting what you watch, through its apps, HDMI and antenna alike. It does not remove the ads in the home screen: only Android TV and Google TV let an owner switch off the apps behind them, which is what OwnYourTV does.
